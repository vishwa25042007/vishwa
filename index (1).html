Be an Earth System Trend Detective

Satellites and global models measure hundreds of variables. This tool helps you choose one, look at a region, draw its time series, and answer four questions: what is changing, where, how much, and is it statistically significant?

1 · SELECT

Answer two questions and get matching NASA variables with plain-language definitions.

2 · LOCATE

Get a region-specific data recipe for NASA Giovanni, or use the built-in demo regions.

3 · VISUALISE

Plot the time series with trend lines, monthly or annual averaging, and seasonal-cycle removal.

4 · TEST

Linear regression with 95% confidence interval and t-test, Mann-Kendall test, and Sen's slope.

Get your own data in five steps

  1. Open NASA GES DISC Giovanni (giovanni.gsfc.nasa.gov) and choose the plot type Time Series, Area-Averaged.
  2. Draw or type a bounding box for your region and pick a date range (several years or more; the longer the better).
  3. Select a variable, for example monthly GPM IMERG precipitation, MODIS aerosol optical depth, OMI NO₂ or MERRA-2 temperature.
  4. Plot it, then download the result as CSV. A NASA Earthdata login may be requested.
  5. Upload that CSV in the Detective Lab above. The parser skips Giovanni's header lines and reads the date and value columns.
